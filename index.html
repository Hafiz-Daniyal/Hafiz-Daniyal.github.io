01 / About

Where industry meets the classroom

I'm a Project Coordinator at F Tech Solutions and a Junior Lecturer at Iqra University. My work mixes shipping real software with teaching others how to build it.

I started with WordPress and C# desktop applications, then moved to native Android (Java, Kotlin) and cross-platform Flutter (Dart). Today I also design agentic AI applications with n8n, AI agents and RAG pipelines.

In the classroom I teach Data Structures and Algorithms, Operating Systems, Object Oriented Programming, Python and Android development, and I enjoy turning complex ideas into hands-on labs.
